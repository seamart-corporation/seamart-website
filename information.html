Rules

# Be Respectful

Treat all members with respect and kindness. No hate speech, discrimination, or harassment will be tolerated.

# No Spam

Do not flood channels with excessive messages or advertisements. Keep self-promotion to designated channels, if allowed.

# Stay On Topic

Keep conversations in the appropriate channels. Avoid derailing discussions with unrelated topics.

# No Trolling or Flaming

Do not engage in trolling or flame wars. Respect differing opinions and engage in constructive discussions.

# Multiple Accounts

Using multiple accounts to evade bans or disrupt the server is not allowed.

# Listen to Moderators and Admins

Follow instructions given by server staff, and respect their decisions.

# No NSFW Content

No NSFW content is allowed. Sending links or videos of NSFW content is strictly prohibited.

# Follow Discord TOS

It is required at all times to follow the official Discord Terms of Service (TOS).
